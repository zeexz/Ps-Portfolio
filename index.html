I am G.P. Hemachandra, an HND Information Technology student at SLIIT City Uni. This portfolio is a record of my journey through the Professional Skills module — reflecting on each session, the activities we conducted, and the growth I experienced.

Through this module, I developed a deeper understanding of communication, professional ethics, emotional intelligence, interview readiness, and negotiation — skills that complement my technical training and prepare me for a successful career in the IT industry.

What I Did & Learned

Session-by-session reflections covering activities, learnings and future applications. Click any card to explore.

S1

Introduction & Goal Setting

Activity: Self-assessment quiz and group discussion on career goals.

Learned: Importance of setting SMART goals and understanding my strengths and areas for improvement.

Future Application: Will use goal-setting frameworks throughout my career planning.

S2

Effective Communication

Activity: Pair speaking exercises, active listening practice and group feedback sessions.

Learned: How to tailor messages for different audiences and the power of non-verbal cues.

Future Application: Apply structured communication in team meetings and client interactions.

S3

Grooming & Etiquette

Activity: Dress code workshop and role-play of professional introductions and greetings.

Learned: Professional appearance directly affects how others perceive competence and credibility.

Future Application: Maintain professional presentation in interviews and workplace settings.

S4

Emotional Intelligence

Activity: EQ self-assessment test and group reflection on emotional triggers.

Learned: Managing emotions leads to better teamwork, less conflict and stronger leadership.

Future Application: Use empathy and self-regulation in team projects and challenging situations.

S5

CV Writing

Activity: Drafted and peer-reviewed personal CVs and LinkedIn summaries.

Learned: How to present skills and experience clearly, concisely and compellingly to employers.

Future Application: Submit a polished CV for internship and graduate job applications.

S6

Interview Skills

Activity: Mock interviews with real-time feedback from peers and the lecturer.

Learned: STAR technique gives structure and confidence when answering competency questions.

Future Application: Use STAR format in all future interviews across technical and professional roles.

S7

Professional Ethics

Activity: Case study analysis of ethical dilemmas in IT and computing.

Learned: As an IT professional, I have a responsibility to uphold integrity, protect data and act honestly.

Future Application: Apply ethical principles in software decisions, data handling and team leadership.

S8

Business Ethics & CSR

Activity: Discussion of corporate responsibility and planning for the CSR initiative.

Learned: Businesses and individuals have a duty to contribute positively to society beyond profit.

Future Application: Champion ethical and socially responsible practices wherever I work.

S9

Negotiation & Conflict

Activity: Role-play negotiation scenarios and group conflict resolution exercises.

Learned: Win-win negotiation preserves relationships while reaching mutually beneficial outcomes.

Future Application: Apply negotiation skills in salary discussions, team disputes and project scoping.

S10

Presentation Skills

Activity: Group presentation delivered to class, peer and lecturer assessment.

Learned: Structured storytelling and clear visuals significantly improve audience engagement.

Future Application: Deliver confident technical and non-technical presentations in professional settings.

S11

Grow Green CSR Event

Activity: Organised and executed the Grow Green plant adoption event for SLIIT community.

Learned: Real-world teamwork and event planning are far more complex and rewarding than classroom simulations.

Future Application: Bring the same community-oriented mindset to future corporate social responsibility initiatives.

Snake Plant & Grow Green Initiative

As part of the Professional Skills CSR programme, our student group collected contributions from fellow students and used them to provide plants for free. Lecturers and students participated in the event and collected plants, creating a joyful and meaningful community experience on campus.

The activity created awareness about sustainability, teamwork and the value of contributing to the university community. Through the event, I gained hands-on experience in event planning, communication, and social responsibility.

This CSR programme demonstrated that professional skills extend far beyond the workplace. Giving plants to lecturers and students was a meaningful way to promote environmental awareness while building genuine collaboration among students and staff.

Final Reflection

My Conclusion

"

The Professional Skills module opened my eyes to the full scope of what it means to be a professional. As an IT student, my focus had always been on coding, systems and software — yet this module revealed that the truly impactful professionals combine technical excellence with strong communication, ethical awareness and emotional intelligence.

The Emotional Intelligence session was particularly eye-opening. Understanding how to manage my own emotions while empathising with others has changed how I approach teamwork and conflict resolution.

The Grooming & Etiquette and Interview Skills sessions gave me immediately actionable tools for presenting myself with confidence. Sessions on Code of Ethics and Business Ethics reinforced my sense of responsibility as a future computing professional — especially regarding data privacy, system integrity, and the societal impact of the software we build.

This portfolio itself is proof of growth. It represents my ability to reflect, organise, and present evidence of learning in a clear and professional manner. I am leaving this module more career-ready, more confident and more aware of the professional standards I must uphold throughout my career.

G.P. Hemachandra HND Information Technology SLIIT City Uni · 2026 Professional Skills Portfolio
